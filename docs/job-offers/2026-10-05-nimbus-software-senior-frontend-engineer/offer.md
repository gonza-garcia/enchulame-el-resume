## Job description

**About Nimbus Software**

Nimbus Software builds real-time analytics dashboards for energy retailers. Our
customers monitor consumption, pricing and alerts from a single web product used by
operations teams all day long.

**The role**

We are hiring a Senior Frontend Engineer to own product surfaces end to end, from the
component library to the release pipeline.

What you will do:

*   Own dashboard surfaces end to end: design, implementation, testing and release.
*   Keep our real-time analytics views fast and predictable under heavy data updates.
*   Evolve the shared component library and its documentation.
*   Raise the testing bar: unit, integration and end-to-end coverage with CI gates.
*   Lead technical discussions and mentor mid-level engineers through code review.

What we look for:

*   5+ years of professional frontend experience with React and TypeScript.
*   Experience with data-heavy interfaces: tables, charts, streaming updates.
*   Strong opinions on state management and data fetching, with the trade-offs to back
    them.
*   A record of improving quality: testing, accessibility, performance budgets.
*   Clear written English; the team works async across four time zones.

Nice to have:

*   Real-time UIs over WebSockets or server-sent events.
*   Design system ownership across teams.
*   Experience mentoring and running frontend guilds.

## Our hiring process

1.  Screening call.
2.  Frontend system discussion (your past work, our current problems).
3.  Team fit conversation and offer.
